# Free Vision Board Maker &amp; Phone Wallpaper Generator

**▶ Use it live: https://pittner.github.io/free-vision-board-maker/**

A free, private, no-signup tool to make a **digital vision board** and a matching **phone lock-screen wallpaper** — right in your browser. Nothing is uploaded; every photo you add is processed locally on your device.

## What it does

- ✍️ Type your goal in your own words
- 🖼️ Add your own photos (optional) — arranged into a board automatically
- 🎨 Pick a colour mood (6 warm-neutral palettes)
- 📱 Export a **vision board** (2:3) *and* a **phone wallpaper** (1080×1920) with your goal + one next step
- 🔒 100% client-side — no account, no server, no watermark

## Why a wallpaper?

You unlock your phone 80–150 times a day. A vision board hidden in a folder does nothing — the same board on your lock screen becomes impossible to ignore. This tool is built around three things that make boards actually work: your **own words**, **daily visibility** (lock screen), and **one concrete next step**.
